05 — BOOKS IDEAS IN PRINT

SCIENCE, PHILOSOPHY & THE QUESTIONS BETWEEN THEM.

WORDS THAT
STAY.

Two books, approaching different questions about intelligence, emotion, memory, and the self.

01 / ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Affective Computing: A Symphony for Man’s Contours

Can a machine understand what a person feels, or can it only recognise patterns that resemble emotion? This book explores how AI reads sentiment through text, faces, and voices—and where those predictions can fail. Moving between research, real-world examples, privacy, bias, and the human need to feel understood, it considers what happens when technology meets the complexity of being human.

SCIENCE × PHILOSOPHY
02 / PHILOSOPHY

The Stranger Called Me

The Stranger Called Me is a brilliant philosophical inquiry exploring memory, personal change, and human identity. It bridges complex theories with everyday experiences, diving deep into how minds reconstruct the past. This captivating text beautifully challenges readers to discover what truly anchors our evolving sense of self across time.
